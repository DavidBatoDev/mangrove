The idea

Above the waterline is the promise. Below it is the evidence.

A mangrove stands because of what you can't see from the boat. A funding promise is the same: it is only as strong as the evidence holding it up. So we keep that evidence visible, dated and traceable, and we lock the promise in public before the money moves. Our one motif is the waterline: promise above, evidence below.

What we are

A public record of mangrove restoration funding promises, with the evidence beside each one. Companies fund restoration and announce the planting. We make the promise public before the money moves, then show everyone whether it came true.

What we are not

A certifier, a funding approver, a success score or ranking, a marketplace, a carbon-credit verifier, or proof of land title or permits. And never “just a map”: maps show data; AIDE-M locks a promise before the money moves and checks it afterwards.

Seven keywords

One vocabulary, from the money to the follow-through.

Use these exact words in UI, copy and decks. Each one is tied to a real part of the product. Read left to right: restoration is why the money moves; evidence is gathered and kept traceable; a baseline is frozen when the promise is locked; every number carries a confidence; then follow-through checks what happened.

1Restoration

What the money is for: bringing mangroves back to a Manila Bay site by natural regeneration, hydrological repair or planting.

"A restoration promise for 8 ha at Pamarawan."not reforestation tree planting greening offsetting
2Evidence

A dated item with its source, version, method, resolution and limitation.

"3 evidence items. Latest observed 28 Sep 2026."not data info proof insights
3Traceable

Every finding, status and number links back to its evidence items. Nothing appears that a source didn’t say.

"Every status is traceable to its sources."not transparent verified trusted
4Baseline

What was known when the promise was locked: the site, every evidence item and every status, frozen.

"Baseline locked 4 Oct 2026 with 4 evidence items."not starting point before
5Promise

What the funder commits to before the money moves. Locked in public; it cannot be edited through AIDE-M.

"The promise: 8 ha of hydrological repair, work check after 1 Apr 2027."not goal KPI target pledge
6Confidence

How exact a number can be shown: High, Medium or Low. Statuses don’t have one; the evidence behind them does.

"Mapped area 5.0 ha, high confidence."not certainty accuracy score rating
7Follow-through

The two later checks: did the work happen, and did the mangroves come back?

"Follow-through: work check after 1 Apr 2027."not monitoring audit impact report

Voice and tone

A field ecologist reading the public record aloud.

To a funding committee and to the town hall. Precise, plain, unhurried, receipts attached. Warm about the coast, cool about the numbers. The voice never changes; the tone shifts with the moment.

"Here’s what was promised, what the evidence says, and what nobody knows yet."

Show the source. If a sentence makes a claim, it carries a date and a source: the evidence item on product screens (Global Mangrove Watch · 2020), a register id like [R04] in decks. Every number names its EQ-### and confidence.
Name the uncertainty. Say what we don’t know: missing evidence, known unknowns, a Low-confidence number shown as a direction. Never hide it, never dramatise it.
People decide. We show and record. Copy leaves the decision with the funder, and the record never enforces anything; it makes deviations visible.
Plain words, short sentences. Grade 8 on public pages and in marketing, grade 10 inside funder and partner screens. Sentence case, verb-plus-object buttons, no exclamation marks.
Say what the record is not. Every record page shows: “This record is not a certification of restoration success or approval of funding.”

Status, confidence and pins

Three different things, each a mark and a word.

Status says what the evidence says together. Confidence says how exact a number can be shown. Pin state says where a record stands on the map. Never mixed, never color alone, never traffic lights, never a score. Red is kept for one thing: a conflict.

Illustration

Drawn by hand.

Bold marker outlines, pale fills, one quick white highlight per leaf and a single chartreuse pop. Drawn from the coast itself: leaf sprigs, saplings in water, root tangles, channels, propagules. They warm up empty states, onboarding, 404s and decks, and never appear on a record, dossier or comparison.

Marker rules

InkOne bold outline per subject: leaf green #2F6B3A for plants, Prop Root for roots, Tidal for water. Round caps, slightly uneven, like a real marker.
FillPale tint inside each shape (#E3EBA6 for leaves, #BFE3DE for water). Never a gradient.
HighlightOne or two quick white streaks inside each leaf or root.
PopA single chartreuse dot with a Canopy ring: a bud, a seed tip, the sun.
LeavesLong, pointed mangrove leaves, opening in fans at the branch tips.
Setsprig · sapling · tangle · propagule · channel · shoreline
STYLE: Hand-drawn marker illustration, like confident sketches in a coastal ecologist's notebook. Bold, slightly uneven marker outlines with round ends; pale flat fills inside each shape; one or two quick white highlight streaks inside every leaf, root and water shape. Palette: leaf green #2F6B3A outlines with pale chartreuse #E3EBA6 fills, brown #7A4E33 roots and trunks, turquoise #2B8C86 and pale #BFE3DE water strokes, soft lavender #9DA6C6 hills, one small chartreuse #C8D545 dot as the only accent. Plain cool off-white #F4F7F5 background. A loose waterline often crosses the scene; mangrove leaves are long and pointed and prop roots are visible. Calm, warm, human. Lots of empty space. Small hand-lettered-looking mono labels are allowed.
NEGATIVE: no gradients, no shading, no 3D, no photorealism, no thin technical linework, no thick black outlines, no faces or characters, no neon, no purple, no text paragraphs, no logos, no hands holding seedlings, no globe, no lightbulb, no smiling sun.
